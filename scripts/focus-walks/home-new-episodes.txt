# Home's New Episodes row (the new episode alerts plan), TV emulator, profile main. Needs a show with
# a new episode (see docs/RUN_GUIDE.md → Focus walks; the plan's emulator walk tells how to make one).
# Written by hand, not recorded yet: record it with scripts/tv-focus-walk.sh -r once a show has new
# episodes. Start: a fresh launch, main picked, with the banner already told, so focus opens on a
# Continue Watching or Channels card; D-pad Down until the TV Shows group.
#
# The row is the first of the TV Shows group: "New Episodes", then "Favourite Shows". Right walks
# its cards; the card's second line says S02E05, N new episodes or New season. OK opens the show
# page on the season of the first new episode; Back returns to the card.
@start Continue Watching card
DOWN	TV Shows group, the New Episodes row's first card
RIGHT	the row's next card
LEFT	the row's first card
DOWN	Favourite Shows row's first card
UP	the New Episodes row's first card
CENTER	the show page, on the season of the first new episode
BACK	the same New Episodes card

## 2025-03-02 - Explicit Semantic Roles for Compose Structural Clickables
**Learning:** In Jetpack Compose, wrapping custom interactive elements (like expandable accordion sections, radio groups, or list toggles) in standard structural components (like `Row`, `Card`, or `Column`) and making them clickable using `Modifier.clickable {}` does not automatically convey the control's specific interaction type to assistive technologies (like Android's TalkBack). By default, a screen reader may just announce "double tap to activate" without context.
**Action:** Always explicitly declare the semantic role using `Modifier.clickable(role = Role.[Type])` (e.g., `Role.Button`, `Role.RadioButton`, `Role.Checkbox`) when applying touch targets to structural containers to ensure accessibility parity with native components.

# Code of Conduct

Fijerena is an open project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews and commit messages) must be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, body,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, social or economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What We Expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people who take part use English as a second or third language.
- Respect different opinions and experience.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person, and accept feedback in the same way.
- When you make a mistake, accept responsibility and learn from it.
- Think about what is best for the project and for its users.

## What Is Not Acceptable

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention of any kind.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing the private information of other people, such as a home or email address, without
  their permission.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would think is not correct in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions. It also applies when a person speaks for the project in public.

## Reports

Tell the maintainer about conduct that is not acceptable. To report in private, use the
repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/mpilasy/fijerena/security) → **Report a
vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainer can see it. If the
matter concerns the maintainer, say so in your report. Reports are kept private, and the
maintainer will not take action against a person who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not obey this code, and can ban a person from the project for a time or for all time. In
most cases the first step is a private warning that says what was wrong. Serious or repeated
problems can cause a ban without a warning.
